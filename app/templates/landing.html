How VisionAttend AI Works

A multi-stage mathematical computer vision pipeline designed for edge reliability.

01

Detection & Alignment

YuNet deep convolutional detector identifies facial bounds and extracts 5 ocular/oral landmarks to perform affine transformation into canonical pose.

02

Anti-Spoof Defense

Filters photo prints with Laplacian edge variance and screens LCD/OLED mobile screens using 2D FFT frequency spectrum moiré analysis.

03

Hypersphere Matching

Transforms aligned crops into 128-dimensional unit feature vectors. Performs rapid Cosine similarity matching against an in-RAM gallery cache.

04

Shift State Machine

Automatically computes On-Time vs Late punctuality, prevents duplicate records with a 5-minute cooldown guard, and manages check-out.
